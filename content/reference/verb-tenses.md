The six tenses used across this site, each with the same sentence carried through it:
**Tú sufres con el calor** — "You suffer in the heat."

*sufrir* is a fully regular `-ir` verb, so these forms are the class pattern rather than
facts about this verb. For the full six-person paradigm of each tense see
[sufrir](/reference/sufrir/); for why the endings work this way, see
[verb classes](/explanation/verb-classes/).

Spanish *con el calor* is literally "with the heat". English prefers "in the heat", and the
literal column below is kept only to show where the two languages part.

## Presente simple — simple present

States what is true now, what is habitual, or what happens as a rule. It carries no sense
of the action being under way at this moment.

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tú + verbo en presente | Tú sufres con el calor. | You suffer with the heat. | You suffer in the heat. |

## Presente continuo — present progressive

States what is under way right now. Built from *estar* plus the gerundio, so both parts
change: *estar* takes the person, the main verb stays in `-iendo`.

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tú estás + gerundio | Tú estás sufriendo con el calor. | You are suffering with the heat. | You are suffering in the heat. |

## Futuro simple — simple future

States what will happen. The endings attach to the whole infinitive rather than to a
stripped stem, so `-ar`, `-er`, and `-ir` verbs share one set of future endings.

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| infinitivo + terminación de futuro | Tú sufrirás con el calor. | You will suffer with the heat. | You will suffer in the heat. |

## Futuro perifrástico — periphrastic ("going to") future

States what is going to happen, usually soon. It is the ordinary spoken future; the futuro
simple is the more formal or more distant of the two.

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| tú vas a + infinitivo | Tú vas a sufrir con el calor. | You are going to suffer with the heat. | You are going to suffer in the heat. |

## Pretérito indefinido — preterite (simple past)

States a past action or situation seen as finished — bounded, over, treated as a single
completed event.

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| verbo en pretérito | Tú sufriste con el calor. | You suffered with the heat. | You suffered in the heat. |

## Pretérito imperfecto — imperfect

States a past action or situation seen as unbounded — habitual, repeated, or still in
progress at the time being described. English renders it as "used to" or "was -ing".

| Formación | Español | English, literal | English, natural |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| verbo en imperfecto | Tú sufrías con el calor. | You were suffering with the heat. | You used to suffer in the heat. |

## Comparación rápida

| Tiempo | Tense | Español | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Presente simple | Simple present | Tú sufres con el calor. | You suffer in the heat. |
| Presente continuo | Present progressive | Tú estás sufriendo con el calor. | You are suffering in the heat. |
| Futuro simple | Simple future | Tú sufrirás con el calor. | You will suffer in the heat. |
| Futuro perifrástico | Periphrastic future | Tú vas a sufrir con el calor. | You are going to suffer in the heat. |
| Pretérito indefinido | Preterite | Tú sufriste con el calor. | You suffered in the heat. |
| Pretérito imperfecto | Imperfect | Tú sufrías con el calor. | You used to suffer in the heat. |
