Spanish Lessons is a personal collection of Spanish lessons, guides, conjugation tables,
and grammar notes, organized by what you are trying to do at the time — learn something
new, get a task done, look a form up, or understand why the language works the way it
does.

## Built with

This site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), a static site generator, and themed
with [Hextra](https://imfing.github.io/hextra/).
