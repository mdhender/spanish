Directions for reaching a specific goal: ordering a meal, introducing yourself, writing a
formal email, asking for directions.

How-to guides are for **working**. They assume you already have some Spanish and know what
you are trying to do. If you are still finding your feet, start with the
[tutorials](/tutorials/) instead.
